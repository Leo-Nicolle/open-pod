OpenPod Sync ​

Fill your Openpod's SD card with the music on this computer, right from the browser. Pick your music folder and the card; the page shows what the card is missing, converts what the player can't play (hi-res FLAC, WAV, AIFF, ALAC) and copies it. Syncing only ever adds music: albums are removed from the card only when you ask, and that is remembered for every computer you sync from.

Everything runs in this tab and your music is never uploaded. It works in Chrome and Edge (other browsers can't write to a folder you pick); the desktop app does the same everywhere and can also convert AAC, Ogg and Opus files.
