STM32F446RE (LQFP64) pinout

Complete 64-pin table — pin number, type, functions and Nucleo-F446RE board mapping.

Interactive pinout

Source: ST DS10693 Rev 10 (STM32F446xC/E datasheet) and UM1724 Rev 16 (Nucleo user manual). 64-pin LQFP64 package, pin 1 at the top-left corner, numbered counter-clockwise.

Alternate functions are selected through the GPIOx_AFR registers; additional functions are enabled directly through peripheral registers (ADC, DAC, oscillator, wake-up, …). Arduino and Morpho columns give the Nucleo-F446RE header pin names. Peripheral bus speeds are in docs/public/pinout/buses.json.
