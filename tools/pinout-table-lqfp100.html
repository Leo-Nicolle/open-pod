Complete 100-pin table — pin number, type, functions.

Interactive pinout

Source: ST DS10693 Rev 10 (STM32F446xC/E datasheet), Table 10. 100-pin LQFP100 package, pin 1 at the top-left corner, numbered counter-clockwise.

Alternate functions are selected through the GPIOx_AFR registers; additional functions are enabled directly through peripheral registers (ADC, DAC, oscillator, wake-up, …). Peripheral bus speeds are in docs/public/pinout/buses.json.
